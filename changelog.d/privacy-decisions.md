### Privacy notice decisions

- Changed: the user guide site no longer shows the repository's star count and latest version
  in its header, so visiting it no longer makes your browser ask `api.github.com`; the header
  keeps a plain link to the repository, and the edit links stay (#627).
- Changed: the privacy notice gives the legal basis for the site's hosting logs, says which
  providers may process data in the US and on what terms, describes how email to
  privacy@browsitory.com is kept and deleted, and no longer claims to meet specific laws (#627).
